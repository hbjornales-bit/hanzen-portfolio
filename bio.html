Biography

Zen is a Filipino filmmaker and communication practitioner whose work spans film production, research, and creative documentation. A Communication and Media Arts graduate, cum laude, from the University of the Philippines Mindanao, she has worked across directing, cinematography, editing, and production coordination, with short films recognized by local film festivals and screening initiatives.

Zen's interests extend beyond film production to the preservation, documentation, and accessibility of Filipino cinema. Her undergraduate research developed a digital film archive prototype through participatory communication, engaging members of the Davao film community in exploring community-driven approaches to film archiving. In 2026, she presented this research as an EngageMedia Fellow and panel speaker at the Cinemata Asia-Pacific Community Convening.

Her practice is grounded in a continuing interest in the people, processes, and communities that shape Filipino cinema.
